# Facts: source of truth (TEMPLATE)

Every number on every résumé comes from here, derived figures included. Fill it once, cite it everywhere, and let no
bullet carry a figure this file does not. Tag each line:

| Tag | Meaning | Sendable? |
|---|---|---|
| `[CONFIRMED]` | documented, or stated by the applicant in writing | yes |
| `[ESTIMATE]` | the applicant's own approximation, hedged inside the sentence ("about", "roughly") | yes, with the hedge intact |
| `⟨OPEN⟩` | nothing supplies this yet | **no: resolve first** |

`grep -c '⟨OPEN⟩'` on the banks tells you how much is left. Nothing under "On the résumé now" may be ⟨OPEN⟩.
